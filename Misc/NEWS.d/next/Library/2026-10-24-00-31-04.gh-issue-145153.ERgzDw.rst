Add the pretty print protocol of :pep:`813`.  Classes can describe their
pretty printed form with a :meth:`~object.__pprint__` method, and the new
:class:`pprint.Printer` class lays out those descriptions.  The built-in and
standard library types, :func:`dataclasses.dataclass` classes and
:func:`collections.namedtuple` classes implement :meth:`!__pprint__`.
:class:`pprint.PrettyPrinter` is now :term:`soft deprecated`.
